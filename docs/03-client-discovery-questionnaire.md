# Primegala: Client Discovery Meeting Pack

**Purpose:** Confirm facts for the marketing site, collect brand assets, and scope the HMIS/ERP so we can quote and plan the modules.
**Prepared by:** Steff Cloud Limited
**Bring:** Laptop with the demo site (`pnpm dev`), this checklist (printed), a phone to photograph documents and signage.

---

## 0. Open the meeting with the demo (10 min)

Show them, in this order:

1. **Homepage on a phone.** Point out the bottom bar (Call, WhatsApp, Book, Directions), the 24-hour badge, "SHA accepted", and the "six miles from town" story.
2. **A service page** (Maternity): story, what to expect, SHA note, FAQs and the booking form.
3. **The Health Hub:** 18 ready-to-publish articles and tips, each awaiting their clinician's review.
4. **Book a visit:** submit a test request and explain that it goes straight into the HMIS as a structured record.
5. **Policies:** Privacy Policy, Patient Rights and Complaints, all written for Kenyan law.

> Talking point: *"Families in Bahati and Kabatini search 'hospital near me' on their phones at night. This site makes sure Primegala is the answer, and every enquiry lands in your system as a record, not a lost phone call."*

## 3. Marketing goals

1. What are the top 3 services you want **more** patients for? (e.g. maternity, lab, chronic clinic)
2. Roughly how many patients a day now? Target in 6 months?
3. Where do patients come from today? (Word of mouth, signage, referrals, churches, schools, chamas, Facebook?)
4. Who are your main competitors in the area? (Our research: Esther Memorial Maternity & Nursing Home on the same road; Kenlands Health Services Maili Sita; Bahati Sub-County Hospital at Maili Kumi; Nakuru Specialist Hospital at Lanet; private hospitals in town.)
5. What makes Primegala different, in your words?
6. Languages: is a Kiswahili version needed now, or later? Kikuyu?
7. Monthly budget for ads (Google, Meta), SMS and WhatsApp messaging?
8. Who will approve content and **clinically review** Health Hub articles? (Required for E-E-A-T and our Editorial Policy.)
9. Who answers WhatsApp and website leads at night? What response time can they commit to?
10. Community activities we can feature: medical camps, school health days at Kiamaina Primary, church partnerships?

## 4. HMIS / ERP discovery (the main system)

### 4.1 Current state
1. **What system do you use now** for patient records, billing and SHA claims? (Paper, Excel, SHA portal, a vendor HMIS? Which one?)
2. **Is it DHA-certified and integrated with the national HIE (AfyaLink)?** SHA's deadline for contracted facilities to use a certified, integrated HMIS was **30 September 2026**. Where do you stand?
3. How are SHA claims submitted today? Rejection rate? Payment delays?
4. Internet at the facility: provider, speed, outages? Power backup?
5. Devices: how many computers, tablets, printers? Barcode or label printers?

### 4.2 People & workflow
6. Staff numbers by cadre (front desk, clinicians, nurses, lab, pharmacy, accounts, admin).
7. Walk through a patient visit, from gate to exit. Where are the queues?
8. Shifts: how does the night shift work? Who covers which desk?
9. Which reports do you submit to the county / MoH (KHIS: MOH 705A/B, 711, 717, 731)? Who compiles them, and how long does it take?
