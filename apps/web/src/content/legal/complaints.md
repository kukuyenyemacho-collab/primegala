Your feedback helps us improve. Whether it's a compliment, a suggestion or a complaint, we want to hear it. Raising a concern will **never** affect the care you receive.

## How to give feedback or complain

- **In person:** speak to the nurse in charge or the facility manager at any time, day or night.
- **Phone or WhatsApp:** {{PHONE}}
- **Email:** {{EMAIL}}
- **Online:** use our [contact form](/contact) and choose "General enquiry".
- **Suggestion box:** at our reception.

Please include your name, contact details, the date of your visit and what happened. You may complain on behalf of someone else with their consent.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement:** we acknowledge your complaint within **2 working days**.
2. **Investigation:** the facility manager reviews records and speaks to the people involved, keeping your information confidential.
3. **Response:** we aim to give you a full response within **14 days**. If we need longer, we'll tell you why and when to expect it.
4. **Learning:** we record every complaint and use it to improve our services.

## If you're not satisfied

You may escalate your concern to the relevant body:

| Concern | Body |
|---|---|
| Conduct of a doctor or dentist, or licensing of the facility | Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC) |
| Conduct of a clinical officer | Clinical Officers Council |
| Conduct of a nurse or midwife | Nursing Council of Kenya |
| Pharmacy or medicines | Pharmacy and Poisons Board |
| Laboratory services | Kenya Medical Laboratory Technicians and Technologists Board |
| Personal data and privacy | Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) |
| SHA eligibility, benefits or claims | Social Health Authority (SHA), through its official channels |
| Public health concerns | Nakuru County Department of Health |

You also retain your right to seek legal advice and remedies under Kenyan law.

## Compliments

If someone at Primegala made a difference to your care, tell us. We'll make sure they hear it.
