Health information affects real decisions. This policy explains how {{NAME}} creates and maintains the content in our Health Hub and across this website.

## Our principles

- **Accurate:** based on current guidance from Kenya's Ministry of Health, the World Health Organization and peer-reviewed evidence.
- **Local:** written for families in Nakuru County, with Kenyan medicines, foods, services and the SHA system in mind.
- **Clear:** plain language, answer-first, with key facts in tables and checklists.
- **Honest:** no exaggerated claims, no promised outcomes and no criticism of other providers, in line with the **Medical Practitioners and Dentists (Practitioners and Health Facilities) (Advertising) Rules, 2016**.
- **Independent:** we don't accept payment or sponsorship for health content, and we don't promote specific drug brands.

## How content is created

1. **Topic selection:** based on the questions our patients ask most often.
2. **Drafting:** by our team, sometimes with the help of digital writing tools, including AI assistants.
3. **Clinical review:** every article is reviewed by a registered health professional before it is marked "Clinically reviewed". The reviewer's name and the review date appear on the article.
4. **Publication:** with the publication date clearly shown.

## Keeping content up to date

- Every health article is reviewed **at least every 12 months**, or sooner if national guidelines change.
- SHA-related content is checked whenever SHA publishes new benefit rules.
- Updated articles show the date of the most recent change.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, please email {{EMAIL}}. We'll review it promptly and correct confirmed errors.

## Patient stories and images

We will only share a patient's story, name or image with their **written, informed consent**, and they may withdraw it at any time. Scenarios that are illustrative rather than real are clearly labelled.
