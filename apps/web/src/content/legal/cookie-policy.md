## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage. They help websites work and can tell owners how a site is used.

## The cookies we use

| Type | Purpose | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Remember your cookie choice, protect forms against spam and keep the Site secure | No: the Site can't work properly without them |
| **Analytics** | Count visits and see which pages help people, using Google Analytics, with IP anonymisation | **Yes.** Only set if you click "Accept" |
| **Embedded maps** | Google Maps may set cookies when you choose to load the map | **Yes.** The map loads only when you click it |

We do **not** use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## Your choices

- Use the cookie banner to **accept** or **decline** analytics. Declining has no effect on how the Site works.
- To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the banner will appear again.
- You can also block cookies in your browser settings. Strictly necessary features may then stop working.
