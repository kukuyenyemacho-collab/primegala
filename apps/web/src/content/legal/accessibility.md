{{NAME}} is committed to making our website and our facility accessible to everyone, including persons with disabilities, in line with **Article 54 of the Constitution of Kenya** and Kenya's laws on the rights of persons with disabilities.

## Our website

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA**. This includes:

- Text and button colours chosen for strong contrast
- Pages that work with keyboard navigation and screen readers
- Descriptive link text, headings and form labels
- Text that can be resized up to 200% without loss of content
- Pages built to load quickly on mobile data and lower-end phones
- Plain-language content, with key information also summarised in Kiswahili

## Our facility

Please tell us in advance, or on arrival, if you need support. We can help with:

- Assistance moving around the facility
- Priority seating while you wait
- A family member, guardian or interpreter accompanying you
- Information explained verbally in English or Kiswahili

## Known limitations

We're continually improving. Some third-party content, such as embedded maps, may not be fully accessible. Contact us and we'll provide the information another way.
