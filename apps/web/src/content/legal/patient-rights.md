At Primegala, every patient is treated with respect and dignity. This charter is based on **Article 43 of the Constitution of Kenya**, the **Health Act, 2017** and the **Kenya National Patients' Rights Charter (Ministry of Health, 2013)**.

## Your rights

As a patient or client of Primegala, you have the right to:

1. **Access health care.** This includes promotive, preventive, curative, reproductive, rehabilitative and palliative care, without discrimination on any grounds.
2. **Emergency treatment.** Under **Article 43(2)** of the Constitution, no one shall be denied emergency medical treatment.
3. **Quality care.** You are entitled to the highest attainable standard of health care products and services from qualified, registered health workers.
4. **Respect and dignity.** You will be treated courteously and with dignity, regardless of age, gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, HIV status, social or economic status.
5. **Information.** You can expect clear information about your condition, the proposed treatment, alternatives, risks and costs, in a language you understand (English or Kiswahili).
6. **Informed consent.** Treatment requires your informed consent, or that of a parent or guardian for a child, except in emergencies where consent cannot be obtained.
7. **Refuse treatment.** You may refuse treatment after being informed of the consequences.
8. **Confidentiality and privacy.** Your health information is confidential under **section 11 of the Health Act, 2017** and the **Data Protection Act, 2019**, and is shared only as the law allows. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
9. **A second opinion.** You may ask for a second medical opinion.
10. **Choose your provider.** You may choose your health care provider where possible.
11. **Access your records.** You may request access to, or a copy of, your medical records.
12. **Complain.** You may give feedback or complain without fear of victimisation. See our [Complaints Procedure](/legal/complaints).

## Your responsibilities

To help us care for you and others, we ask you to:

1. **Take care of your health** by adopting a healthy lifestyle.
2. **Give accurate information** about your health, medicines and history.
3. **Follow the agreed treatment plan,** or tell us if you can't or don't want to.
4. **Treat staff and other patients with respect.** Violence, abuse and harassment of health workers will not be tolerated.
5. **Respect other patients' privacy** and confidentiality.
6. **Keep appointments,** or let us know in good time if you can't attend.
7. **Pay for services** not covered by SHA or insurance, and keep your SHA registration up to date.
8. **Follow facility rules,** including infection prevention and visiting guidelines.
9. **Report** any concerns or unsafe conditions you notice.

## Speak up

If you feel any of your rights have not been respected, please tell the nurse in charge or use our [complaints procedure](/legal/complaints). We will listen and act.
