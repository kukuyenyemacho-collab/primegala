**Short answer:** Sleep under an insecticide-treated net **every night, all year**, tuck it in under the mattress, repair holes quickly, prioritise children and pregnant women, and clear standing water around your home.

1. **Every night, every season.** Mosquitoes bite from dusk to dawn, even when it's cold.
2. **Tuck it in.** Tuck the edges under the mattress so mosquitoes can't get in.
3. **Repair holes.** Sew or tie small holes before they grow.
4. **Protect the most vulnerable first.** Pregnant women and children under five are most at risk of severe malaria.
5. **Clear standing water.** Empty containers, old tyres and blocked drains where mosquitoes breed.
