**Short answer:** Go to a health facility immediately, at any hour, if you are pregnant and have **vaginal bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, convulsions, swelling of the face or hands, fever, severe abdominal pain, your waters breaking early, or your baby moving less than usual.**

Most pregnancies go well. But when something goes wrong, the hours matter. These are the signs listed in Kenya's Mother & Child Health booklet that every pregnant woman, and everyone around her, should know.

## The danger signs

1. **Vaginal bleeding** at any stage of pregnancy.
2. **Severe headache** that doesn't go away, especially with blurred vision.
3. **Blurred vision or seeing spots.** Along with headache, this can signal pre-eclampsia (dangerously high blood pressure).
4. **Swelling of the face and hands.** Some ankle swelling is normal; puffiness of the face and hands is not.
5. **Convulsions (fits)** or loss of consciousness.
6. **Fever,** especially with chills, which may signal an infection or malaria.
7. **Severe abdominal pain** that isn't normal labour.
8. **Waters breaking** (fluid leaking from the vagina) before labour starts or before 37 weeks.
9. **Reduced or no baby movements** after you've started feeling them regularly.

## What to do

- **Don't wait** to "see if it gets better."
- **Go straight to a facility,** or call 999 or 112 for emergencies.
- **Bring your Mother & Child Health booklet,** your ID and your SHA details.
- **Tell the nurse at the door** exactly what's happening so you're seen quickly.

## Plan ahead so you're never stuck

Agree on transport for night-time emergencies *before* you need it: a neighbour with a car, a trusted boda rider's number, or a family member. Save the number of your nearest 24-hour facility in your phone today.
