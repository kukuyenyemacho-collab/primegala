**Short answer:** Primegala Medical Centre is at Maili Sita on the Nakuru–Nyahururu Road, opposite Kiamaina Primary School, and is open 24 hours. Bring your ID, your SHA details and any previous medical records, then walk in or book ahead on WhatsApp.

A first visit anywhere can feel uncertain. Where do I go? Will it take all day? Will SHA work? Here's what happens from the moment you arrive.

## Finding us

- **Address:** Maili Sita Centre, Nakuru–Nyahururu Road, Kabatini Ward, Nakuru North
- **Landmark:** Directly opposite **Kiamaina Primary School**
- **From Nakuru town:** About 10 km north on the Nakuru–Nyahururu Road. "Maili Sita" literally means *six miles*.
- **By matatu:** Any matatu heading towards Bahati, Kiamaina or Nyahururu can drop you at Maili Sita.

[Open directions in Google Maps](/contact)

## What to bring

- National ID (or birth certificate for children)
- The phone number registered with SHA (for verification codes)
- Previous prescriptions, test results or discharge notes
- Your **Mother & Child Health booklet** for pregnancy and child visits
- A list of medicines you currently take
- M-Pesa or cash for anything not covered

## How a visit works

1. **Registration.** Our front desk opens your patient file and checks your SHA status.
2. **Triage.** A nurse measures your temperature, blood pressure, pulse and weight. The sickest patients are always seen first.
3. **Consultation.** A clinician listens to your concerns, examines you and explains what they find in plain language.
4. **Tests.** If you need a test, our on-site laboratory takes your sample. Most routine results are ready the same visit.
5. **Treatment.** Your prescription is filled at our pharmacy, with clear instructions on how to take each medicine.
6. **Follow-up.** If you need a review, we'll agree on a date and can remind you on WhatsApp.

## How long will it take?

Most outpatient visits take under an hour, but waiting times vary with how busy we are and how urgent other patients' needs are. Booking ahead helps us plan.

## Your privacy

What you share with us stays confidential. We handle your information in line with the Health Act, 2017, the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Digital Health Act, 2023. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
