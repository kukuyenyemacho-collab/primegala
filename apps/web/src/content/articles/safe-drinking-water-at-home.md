**Short answer:** Make water safe by **boiling** it (a full rolling boil for at least one minute) or **treating it with chlorine** following the product instructions, then **store it covered** in a clean container and pour it out rather than dipping cups in.

Typhoid, cholera and many cases of diarrhoea spread through contaminated water. These three habits protect your whole family.

## 1. Boil it

Bring water to a full rolling boil for at least one minute. Let it cool covered.

## 2. Treat it

Water treatment products containing chlorine are widely available in Kenyan shops. Use exactly the amount on the label and wait the stated time (usually 30 minutes) before drinking.

## 3. Store it safely

- Use a clean container with a lid and, ideally, a tap.
- Don't dip cups or hands into stored water.
- Clean the container regularly.

## Plus: wash your hands

Wash hands with soap and water after using the toilet, after changing nappies and before preparing or eating food.
