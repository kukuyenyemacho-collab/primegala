**Short answer:** For a child with diarrhoea, give **ORS** (oral rehydration solution) after every loose stool, give **zinc** daily for 10–14 days as advised by a health worker, and **keep feeding and breastfeeding**. Come in immediately if you see danger signs.

## How to use ORS

- Mix one sachet with the exact amount of clean water stated on the packet.
- Give small, frequent sips from a cup or spoon after each loose stool.
- If your child vomits, wait 10 minutes, then continue more slowly.
- Make a fresh mix every 24 hours.

## Why zinc?

Zinc shortens diarrhoea and helps prevent it from coming back in the following months. A health worker will advise on the dose for your child's age.

## Keep feeding

Continue breastfeeding and offer food. Children need nutrients to recover.

## Come in immediately if your child

- Can't drink or breastfeed, or vomits everything
- Has sunken eyes, a very dry mouth or no tears
- Is unusually sleepy or difficult to wake
- Has blood in the stool
- Has diarrhoea for more than 14 days
