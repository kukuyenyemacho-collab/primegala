**Short answer:** Primegala Medical Centre opened in March 2022 at Maili Sita, about six miles (10 km) from Nakuru town on the Nakuru–Nyahururu Road, to give families in Kabatini, Kiamaina, Bahati and the surrounding areas round-the-clock care close to home.

## A name that's also a distance

In Swahili, *Maili Sita* means "six miles": the distance from Nakuru town along the old Nyahururu road. For generations, those six miles were the gap between a family and the nearest hospital.

Six miles doesn't sound far. Then picture it at 2 a.m. A toddler is burning with fever. Matatus have stopped running. A boda ride to town costs money the household doesn't have to spare. Or picture a mother in early labour, unsure whether there's time to get to town.

Those are the moments Primegala was built for.

## Opening the doors in 2022

Primegala Medical Centre opened on **1 March 2022** at Maili Sita Centre, directly opposite **Kiamaina Primary School**. It is a registered **KEPH Level 3** medical centre, and from the start it has kept its doors open **24 hours a day**.

The first services were the ones neighbours asked for most: outpatient consultations, antenatal care for expectant mothers, family planning (including long-acting methods), HIV testing and counselling, and inpatient care for patients who need to stay.

## What "prime care, close to home" means

**Prime care** means doing the basics exceptionally well: listening properly, testing before treating, explaining clearly and treating every person with dignity, whether they arrive at noon or midnight.

**Close to home** means being there when it matters. It means the parents dropping children at Kiamaina Primary can pop in for a blood pressure check. It means a mother-to-be can attend every antenatal visit without losing a day's wages travelling to town. And it means families can visit a loved one on the ward after work.

## Looking ahead

Kenya's health system is changing. The Social Health Authority (SHA) now funds primary care at facilities like ours, and the government is connecting health facilities through digital systems so records and claims move securely.

Primegala is investing in those changes too: a digital patient system that connects to the national health information exchange, WhatsApp reminders so no one misses a vaccine or a review, and this website, so that anyone searching for help at Maili Sita can find clear, honest information.

The six miles haven't changed. What's changed is that you no longer have to travel them for good care.
