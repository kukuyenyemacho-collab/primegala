**Short answer:** If you are registered with the Social Health Authority (SHA), you can use it at Primegala Medical Centre for eligible services. Bring your national ID and the phone registered with SHA, and our front desk will confirm your eligibility before treatment.

Since SHA replaced NHIF in October 2024, we've met many patients at our Maili Sita front desk who weren't sure whether they were registered, what was covered or what to bring. This guide answers those questions in one place.

## Step 1: Make sure you're registered

Every Kenyan resident is expected to register with SHA. You can:

- **Dial \*147#** on any network and follow the prompts.
- **Register online** through the official SHA portal.
- **Ask us.** Our front desk can walk you through registration on your phone.

You'll need your **national ID number**. Children are registered as dependants of a parent or guardian.

> **Tip:** Register your whole household, not just yourself. Babies and spouses must be added as dependants to be covered.

## Step 2: Understand the three SHA funds

SHA pays for care through three funds. Knowing which one applies helps you know what to expect.

| Fund | What it covers | Where it matters at Primegala |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Healthcare Fund (PHCF)** | Outpatient consultations, basic lab tests, antenatal care, family planning, child health and other primary care | Most day-to-day visits |
| **Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF)** | Inpatient care, surgery, maternity beyond primary level, specialist services | Admissions |
| **Emergency, Chronic & Critical Illness Fund (ECCIF)** | Emergencies, critical care and chronic illness beyond SHIF limits | Emergency stabilisation and referral |

Primary care at Level 2 and 3 facilities like Primegala is funded through the **PHCF** for registered members.

## Step 3: Check your status before you need care

Dial **\*147#** and choose the option to check your status. It's much easier to sort out a registration problem on a quiet afternoon than in the middle of the night with a sick child.

## Step 4: What happens at the front desk

When you arrive at Primegala:

1. **Show your ID** (or your child's birth certificate).
2. **We look you up** in the national system to confirm your registration and eligibility.
3. **Verify it's you.** You may receive a one-time code on the phone registered with SHA, so keep that phone with you.
4. **We explain** what SHA covers for your visit and whether anything isn't covered, *before* treatment.

## Step 5: Pregnancy and SHA

If you're expecting, register on SHA as early as possible in your pregnancy and confirm your status at your first antenatal visit. Under current SHA rules, normal delivery at Level 2 and 3 facilities is paid for through the Primary Healthcare Fund for registered members. Benefit rules can change, so we'll always confirm what applies to you well before your due date.

## Common problems (and fixes)

- **"Not found" when we search your ID:** You may not be registered, or your details may differ. Register via \*147# and we'll check again.
- **Your child isn't listed:** Add them as a dependant through \*147# or the SHA portal.
- **Your phone number has changed:** Update it with SHA so you can receive verification codes.
